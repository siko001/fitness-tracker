Your health, your data.

Steady is a personal food and fitness diary. Your food entries, recipes, profile, weight and imported activity are saved in this app on your device. Steady has no advertising or analytics. Device-only use needs no account. You can optionally enable hosted sync with your own Supabase project, or local sync with a server on your computer.

Health access

Only when you select Connect health & enable auto-checks does Steady request permission to read steps, active calories and walking/running distance from Health Connect or Apple Health. Steady reads the last seven local calendar days, stores daily totals, and displays them alongside your food diary. After connection, it reads automatically on opening or returning to Steady and every 30 seconds while open. Automatic checks can be turned off on the Activity page. It does not write to your health store. Android can optionally read these same activity measurements while closed after you enable background checks and grant additional Health Connect background permission. Checks run about every 15 minutes, subject to Android battery scheduling. iPhone imports still require the app to be open. Permission can be revoked in your phone’s health settings.

Your watch manufacturer’s app may have its own account, internet requirements and privacy policy. Steady does not control those services.

Optional device sync

Hosted sync sends your profile, food library, recipes, diary, weight and activity totals over HTTPS to your own Supabase project. It uses Supabase sign-in and access policies that restrict each diary to its owner. Your hosting providers process data under their own policies. This is not end-to-end encryption. Local sync sends the same records to the server you pair using its private key. Home network connections require HTTPS; localhost connections can also work over Android USB forwarding. Diary sync runs while the app is open, after changes and about every 30 seconds. If Android background health checks are enabled, the phone can also upload activity totals while closed using your chosen hosted/local connection. Pending activity and sign-in credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore in app-private non-backup storage; tokens refreshed by the worker are shared with the app. Background work does not upload unsent food or recipe edits. You can turn it off in Settings.

Optional food reminders

The installed phone app can schedule local notifications after you enable them and grant notification permission. No push service or notification account is used. Reminder preferences stay on this device. Android uses a rolling local alarm, repeating about every 20 minutes until a meal is logged or skipped for that date, or the chosen overnight cutoff is reached. Notification Skip actions are saved on Android immediately, then merged into the diary on next app use. iPhone schedules its next 60 checks, refreshed on use; its skip action opens the app to save the choice. Each day starts fresh. Logged and skipped meals sync as part of your diary. Records on another device count after they sync here. Notifications contain a meal label and can offer up to three recent foods or saved recipe portions. Zepp or another notification bridge may display those names and portions on your watch. A supported Reply action saves the selected portion privately on the phone and stops repeats; it joins the diary and device sync when you next open Steady. Test replies do not change your diary. Notifications do not include your weight. Disable reminders in Settings or revoke notification permission in phone settings.

Backup and deletion

You can export your records to a file and choose where to keep it. Backup files contain personal health information and are not encrypted by Steady. Sharing a file sends it only to the location or app you choose. Clear local data in Settings to remove your records from this installation; exported backups, synced server records and your phone’s health store are unaffected. To delete hosted records, remove your diary row or user in your Supabase dashboard. To remove a local server diary, stop the server and remove its data directory. Signing out does not remove local data; clear local data before sharing the device.

Offline storage

The browser version caches the application for offline use after the first successful load. Browser storage may be removed if you clear site data or your device reclaims space, so keep backups. The native app includes its interface and food library. Optional system device backups may include app data.

Food search

General-food search uses a bundled USDA catalog. Packaged-product search sends only your search term or barcode through this app’s hosted/local food-search endpoint to Open Food Facts. Your diary, profile and health data are not included. Your hosting provider may log normal request metadata. Searching does not add a diary entry or edit the public source. You review and save selected foods, which then remain offline and sync with your diary.

Optional Groq food assistant

When you send an AI message, Steady sends the conversation, current proposal and your food/recipe library to your selected Steady server. The server sends the conversation and relevant food matches to Groq to suggest foods and portions. It does not include your profile, weight or activity records. Web menu research is optional and may send the food/restaurant query to Groq’s search providers. Packaged lookups use Open Food Facts. Groq and hosting providers process requests under their own policies; Steady does not log prompts or keys in application logs. A key typed into AI settings remains temporary until you choose Save encrypted key. Saving sends it over HTTPS to the Steady server, which encrypts it with AES-256-GCM before storing it in a separate, account-restricted Supabase table. The encryption secret stays in the server environment. Your signed-in devices can use the key through the server; the plaintext key is never downloaded to them or included in diary backups, conversation history or browser storage. The server can decrypt it to call Groq, so this is encryption at rest, not end-to-end encryption. Replace or remove the saved copy in AI settings. Removing it from Steady does not revoke it at Groq. Unsaved session keys are cleared on sign-out. An optional server environment key may instead be restricted to specified accounts. Suggestions remain unsaved until you review and save. New AI-generated nutrition is labelled estimated. Find list uses saved foods and the bundled catalog without sending an AI request.

Optional nutrition label photos

When you choose Read label, Steady sends up to two resized photos through your selected Steady server to Groq for transcription. Photos are re-encoded on your device without the original location metadata. This request does not include your diary, profile or health activity. Steady does not store these photos or include them in backups or device sync; Groq and hosting providers process requests under their own policies. The extracted draft stays on this browser or installation until saved or replaced. Only the reviewed product, nutrition and any diary entry you save join your library and device sync. Camera and file access start only when you choose Take photo or Upload photo. Missing nutrients are not inferred from a food picture.

Nutrition references

Bundled food reference values are from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, April 2018, and FNDDS 2021–2023 (public domain). Imported packaged data is attributed to Open Food Facts contributors under ODbL, with individual contents under DbCL. Values vary by product and preparation; use your packaging when available.
