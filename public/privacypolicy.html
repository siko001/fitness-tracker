Your health, your data.

Steady is a personal food and fitness diary. Your food entries, recipes, profile, weight and imported activity are saved in this app on your device. Steady has no advertising or analytics. Device-only use needs no account. You can optionally enable hosted sync with your own Supabase project, or local sync with a server on your computer.

Health access

Only when you select Connect health & enable auto-checks does Steady request permission to read steps, active calories and walking/running distance from Health Connect or Apple Health. Steady reads the last seven local calendar days, stores daily totals, and displays them alongside your food diary. After connection, it reads automatically on opening or returning to Steady and every five minutes while open. Automatic checks can be turned off on the Activity page. It does not write to your health store or read health data while closed. Permission can be revoked in your phone’s health settings.

Your watch manufacturer’s app may have its own account, internet requirements and privacy policy. Steady does not control those services.

Optional device sync

Hosted sync sends your profile, food library, recipes, diary, weight and activity totals over HTTPS to your own Supabase project. It uses Supabase sign-in and access policies that restrict each diary to its owner. Your hosting providers process data under their own policies. This is not end-to-end encryption. Local sync sends the same records to the server you pair using its private key. Home network connections require HTTPS; localhost connections can also work over Android USB forwarding. Sync runs while the app is open, after changes and about once a minute. You can turn it off in Settings.

Optional food reminders

The installed phone app can schedule local notifications after you enable them and grant notification permission. No push service or notification account is used. Reminder preferences stay on this device. Android uses a rolling local alarm, repeating about every 20 minutes until a meal is logged or skipped for that date, or the chosen overnight cutoff is reached. Notification Skip actions are saved on Android immediately, then merged into the diary on next app use. iPhone schedules its next 60 checks, refreshed on use; its skip action opens the app to save the choice. Each day starts fresh. Logged and skipped meals sync as part of your diary. Records on another device count after they sync here. Notifications contain a meal label, not your weight or calorie totals. Disable reminders in Settings or revoke notification permission in phone settings.

Backup and deletion

You can export your records to a file and choose where to keep it. Backup files contain personal health information and are not encrypted by Steady. Sharing a file sends it only to the location or app you choose. Clear local data in Settings to remove your records from this installation; exported backups, synced server records and your phone’s health store are unaffected. To delete hosted records, remove your diary row or user in your Supabase dashboard. To remove a local server diary, stop the server and remove its data directory. Signing out does not remove local data; clear local data before sharing the device.

Offline storage

The browser version caches the application for offline use after the first successful load. Browser storage may be removed if you clear site data or your device reclaims space, so keep backups. The native app includes its interface and food library. Optional system device backups may include app data.

Nutrition references

Bundled food reference values are from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, April 2018. Values vary by product and preparation; use your packaging when available.
